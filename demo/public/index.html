RDF Label Cache

Why label-cache? Getting started →

Opaque RDF IRIs become human-readable labels, served from a globally edge-cached CDN. This demo includes labels for RDF, RDFS, OWL, SKOS, DC Terms, DCAT and all of Schema.org (~3,200 terms). Paste Turtle on the left: it renders those labels — plus any labels you provide in your own RDF. An IRI with no label shows as a CURIE (when your Turtle declares a matching prefix) or the full IRI.
